How it works

Three simple steps, from report to resolution

📝

1. Report

Citizens register and submit a complaint with a photo and location.

✅

2. Verify & Assign

GHMC Admin verifies the complaint and assigns it to Field Staff.

🏁

3. Resolve

Field Staff updates progress until the issue is marked Resolved.
